ORE DRIFT

Mine glowing asteroids, upgrade your rig and buy a Warp Drive. Every other prospector online flies in the same belt as you.
How to play
• Steer with A/D or ←/→. Thrust with W/↑, brake with S/↓.
• Hold Space or the mouse button to fire your mining laser. Shatter rocks and fly through the ore crystals to scoop them up.
• Go back to the station (follow the gold arrow) and press E to dock. Docking sells your cargo, repairs your hull and opens the upgrade shop.
• Richer ore lies further out: Cobalt Drift has meteor storms, the Gilded Belt has pirate drones and the Plasmite Abyss has mines.
• Buy the Warp Drive (30,000 cr) to win. P/Esc pauses, M mutes. On a phone, use the stick and the MINE button.
